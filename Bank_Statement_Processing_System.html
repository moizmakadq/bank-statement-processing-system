1. Ingestion & OpenCV + Tesseract OCR

PDFDetector inspects per-page character density and embedded images via pdfplumber & pypdf. Image-based pages are rasterized at 300 DPI using pypdfium2, deskewed via minimum-area bounding boxes in OpenCV, enhanced with CLAHE + Otsu binarization, and parsed with pytesseract (`--psm 6`).

2. Extraction & Balance Reconciliation

StatementExtractor handles both structured grid tables and borderless OCR text lines across Indian & global banks. CleanerAndValidator normalizes dates, corrects OCR O->0 typos, auto-corrects swapped Debit/Credit polarity using running balance deltas, and flags duplicates & reversals.

3. Non-LLM Hybrid Classification Engine

HeuristicRuleClassifier applies 23 priority-weighted regex & merchant rules across 14 categories. TraditionalMLClassifier computes sublinear TF-IDF over word (1,2)-grams & character 4-grams and ensembles Multinomial Naive Bayes with L2 Softmax Logistic Regression.
